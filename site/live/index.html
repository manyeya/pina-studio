Pina Live / Version 0.1.0

DJ on a studio's engine. Pull the track apart mid-mix.

Pina Live is DJ software built on Pina Studio's engine. Keylock that follows the pitch fader, sync that lands on the bar, beat FX that ring out, and stems you can mute while the track plays. Two decks or four, for macOS, Windows and your browser. Free and open source, by Manyeya Khutso Siema.

The decks

Plays like a player. Mixes like a booth.

Drag a track onto the deck you want, and it plays like a player: CUE and PLAY lit as a CDJ's are (PLAY's ring brightening on every beat), a jog with the phrase's sixteen beats round it and, on its screen, the phrase it's in and the bars left of it. Eight pads for hot cues, loops, rolls and jumps; keylock, slip, and sync that lands on the bar, then the beat; MATCH shifts a track's key to the nearest one that mixes with the other deck. The waveforms scroll above, numbered by the bar.

Part a track into vocals, drums, bass and the rest, or just the part you need: vocals only or the beat only goes to the quickest model you have. Tracks are parted as they load, so the stems are usually there when you reach for them. Like Pina Studio, Pina Live ships no models: download one from where its maker hosts it, or add your own.

Four decks: A and C on the crossfader's left, B and D on its right, each end lighting up with how much of it you hear. In a narrower window each side shows one deck at a time, switched between A and C or B and D. The mixer grows with the window, every panel resizes by its edge, and the library closes or fills the window.

Pina Studio's sister app

The studio's engine, made for the booth.

Pina Live doesn't rebuild what Pina Studio already does well. It plays through it: the same stretch, the same ear, the same effects, the same separation. A track made in Pina Studio sounds the same when you play it out.

Keylock

Signalsmith Stretch, following the pitch fader every block, with a key shift of twelve semitones either way.

Beat, bar and key

Pina Studio's song study finds every track's grid, its key and Camelot number, and its phrases: intro, build, drop, outro.

Effects

Pina Studio's echoes, reverbs, flanger, phaser and pitch shifts, timed in beats of the leading deck.

Limiter

Its true-peak limiter on the master, with what it takes off metered on the mixer.

Stems

The separation models you've added to Pina Studio part tracks here too, as they load.

The mix

Everything a set needs. Nothing in the way.

Mix

A three-band isolator EQ and a one-knob filter on every channel, each knob reading what it's set to, meters that hold their peaks, auto gain to −10 LUFS, a crossfader with three curves, headphone cue on a second pair of outputs or split in one jack, and Pina Studio's limiter on the master with what it takes off metered. Record the mix to WAV.

Effects

EchoReverbFlangerTape Stop

Fifteen beat-synced effects in two units, each on any deck, after the fader, with tails that ring out when they're switched off. Each unit shows its length in milliseconds at the tempo, and the bar it's timed against.

Library

Folders, crates and history. Search by name, bpm: or key:, Match for the tracks that mix with a deck in key and tempo, and a preview in the headphones. MIDI learn for any controller.

In your browser

Nothing to install. Open it and mix.

The same app, in a browser tab. Add the folders you keep your music in, drop tracks onto the page, or try the two demo songs: everything's read on your computer, never uploaded, and each track's tempo, key and waveform are found while you play.

Version 0.1.0/Release notes

macOS

Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 13 or later. A disk image with an installer.

Disk image (.dmg)

It isn't notarized yet. If macOS won't open it: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.

Windows

x64. A per-user installer, no administrator needed, or a zip.

Installer (.exe) Zip

The installer isn't code-signed yet. If SmartScreen stops it, choose More info › Run anyway.

In your browser

Nothing to install. Add a folder or drop tracks in: they stay on your computer.
