About Pina

Made by Manyeya Khutso Siema.

Pina Studio and Pina Live are designed and written by Manyeya Khutso Siema: the apps, the audio engine they share, the agents that work in your songs, the installers and this website. Two apps from one engine, one maker, both free.

manyeya.dev github.com/manyeya

What Manyeya Khutso Siema made

One engine. Two apps on it.

Pina Studio

DAW / macOS, Windows

A DAW where Claude Code, Codex or any agent works in your song with you. You play; it writes, arranges, mixes and masters, one undoable edit at a time.

Pina Studio Source

Pina Live

DJ software / macOS, Windows, browser

Two decks or four on Pina Studio's engine: keylock, sync that lands on the bar, beat FX that ring out, and stems you pull apart mid-mix.

Pina Live Source
